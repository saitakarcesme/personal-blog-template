From its music to its realism and animation, this is an incredibly high-quality film. I definitely recommend it.

Another thing I can say about this film is that the story of a king cannot be the story of a purely innocent young boy, and this film explains that beautifully.

A young man who grew up on the streets and faced the harsh reality of life can truly become a king if that is his destiny. Compared to the innocent king stories that feel like they come straight out of children’s tales, I really liked this more realistic approach.
